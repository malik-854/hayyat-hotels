Frequently Asked Questions

Everything About Hayyat Heights

Have questions about your upcoming stay on Jail Road? Here are the answers.

Hayyat Heights is situated at S-77-R-34/1 Jail Road, Lahore. It is positioned right on the main commercial corridor of Jail Road, moments away from Jilani Park, Services Hospital, and Gulberg Main Boulevard.

Standard check-in begins from 14:00 (2:00 PM), and check-out is until 12:00 noon. Early check-in or late check-out is subject to room availability.

We strictly accommodate families, married couples, business professionals, and international travelers. Unmarried couples are not permitted in accordance with property policy. Valid national CNIC or passport is required upon arrival.

Yes, all our one-bedroom apartments, two-bedroom suites, and top-floor suites come with gourmet kitchens equipped with a stovetop, microwave, refrigerator, and cooking utensils. Studio rooms include a convenient kitchenette.

No prepayment is required! You can select your dates and room online to receive an instant PDF confirmation receipt. Payment is made securely upon arrival via Cash or Bank Transfer.
